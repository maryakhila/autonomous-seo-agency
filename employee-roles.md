# SEO Agency – Employee Roles

## 1. Project Manager
Coordinates the team and manages reporting.

## 2. SEO Research Specialist
Researches search trends and market opportunities.

## 3. Keyword Analyst
Finds keywords and evaluates search intent.

## 4. Technical SEO Auditor
Identifies website technical problems.

## 5. SEO Content Writer
Creates search-optimized website content.

## 6. On-Page SEO Specialist
Improves titles, descriptions, headings, and links.

## 7. Competitor and Backlink Analyst
Studies competitors and backlink opportunities.

## 8. SEO Performance Analyst
Measures organic traffic and SEO results.
