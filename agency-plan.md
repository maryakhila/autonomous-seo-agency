# Autonomous SEO Agency

## Project Objective

To develop an autonomous SEO agency using
eight specialized AI employees who collaborate
to improve website search visibility and
organic search performance.

## AI Employee Team

1. Project Manager
2. SEO Research Specialist
3. Keyword Analyst
4. Technical SEO Auditor
5. SEO Content Writer
6. On-Page SEO Specialist
7. Competitor and Backlink Analyst
8. SEO Performance Analyst

## Project Manager Responsibilities

- Coordinate all seven SEO employees.
- Monitor employee activities.
- Record tasks in Google Sheets.
- Send daily reports through Gmail.

## Expected Results

An automated SEO workflow with centralized
task management, reporting, and performance analysis.
